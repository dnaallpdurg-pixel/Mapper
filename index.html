Kaise use karein

Multiple screens: Upar S1, S2... me se surface chuno, + se naya surface jodo. Har surface ke 4 neele circles drag karke us object par fit karo, aur 📂 se uska alag video/image lagao.

Output mode: controls aur circles chhup jate hain. Wapis laane ke liye screen par double-tap karo.

Wireless connect

Windows: Win + K (Miracast), phir Win + P me Extend, aur page ko projector screen par le jao.

Android: Smart View / Cast / Screen Cast on karo.

iPhone/iPad: Control Center se Screen Mirroring (AirPlay).

Projector me wireless nahi hai? HDMI Chromecast, Fire Stick ya Miracast dongle lagao.
